# Summary: Tiny Box Miley Enclosure Ep7 #millenniummachines #opensource #cnc

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is

---

## TL;DR
This is a casual livestream by the creator of Millennium Machines (the open-source CNC project behind the Milo/Miley/Atlas machines), building out the "Casita" enclosure — moving from bare frame to fully paneled box, wiring fans, and running a smoke test of the extraction/filter system. Most of the runtime is chat banter rather than tutorial: community news (a Kickstarter-funded printer called Stonewolf collapsing, a well-known 3D-printing YouTuber stepping back from full-time content), a long riff on how small the UK 3D-printing/CNC market is, and updates on the upcoming Millennium Machines V2 kit and the Prusa-sponsored Atlas machine. There's some concrete fabrication/material info, but it's scattered between video-game talk and Discord shout-outs.

## Key points
- Enclosure build goes from bare frame to fully paneled box; a smoke test confirms the extraction fan and filter clear smoke from the chamber quickly, though smoke leaks from unsealed seams due to over-pressure, meaning intake fan speed needs software tuning [2:02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=7330s), [2:04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=7440s).
- The UK 3D-printing/CNC hobby market is tiny — the host says only ~15 Milo kits sold in the whole country during a past run — so bulk hardware/extrusion deals need roughly 50 sets to be worthwhile, which is hard to organize domestically [1:50:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=6610s), [1:51:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=6660s).
- Millennium Machines V2 kit is imminent: the host's LDO-supplied V2 kit is arriving within days and he'll try to assemble it before public launch; the current print list is current except for LDO's own aesthetic shroud, and V2 swaps printed anti-backlash blocks for LDO's metal ones [1:51:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=6703s), [1:52:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=6723s).
- Atlas, a new larger CNC machine sponsored by Prusa (explaining this build's orange/black color scheme), is in development; the host may bring the finished enclosure to Prague Maker Faire and is considering a UK group order for Atlas parts [23:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=1399s), [1:43:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=6196s), [44:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=2684s).
- A Kickstarter-funded printer called Stonewolf (billet-gantry frame, priced ~€1,300–1,500) has collapsed — the creator reportedly invested backers' money and lost it, and is only refunding recent orders, which the host frames as standard (if painful) Kickstarter risk, not a legal obligation [36:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=2206s), [38:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=2321s).
- Samurai CNC is cited as a parallel cautionary tale: a Kickstarter-funded "affordable" epoxy-granite CNC that failed to deliver but still operates, now surviving mainly on pre-orders for machines (including a promised 5-axis system) that don't yet exist [41:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=2491s), [42:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=2542s).
- A prominent 3D-printing YouTuber/streamer — named in the transcript as "Nero," also called "Connect Creator" (sic, unclear which name is accurate) — announced he's job-hunting and stepping back from full-time YouTubing for personal, not community-related, reasons [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=444s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=484s).
- Host is giving away/selling his Centauri Carbon printer because two dead spots on the bed prevent proper first-layer squish for production parts; he'd already swapped its dead-on-arrival hot-end fan for a Creality K2 fan and suggests a ~£30 "canvas" hotend rebuild as a possible fix [8:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=535s), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=657s), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=807s).
- Explains when to pick Miley vs. Milo vs. Atlas: Miley suits tight spaces, Milo suits hobbyists who want to build/learn a machine and add a 4th axis or tool changer, and Atlas is for large-plate work; switching between Milo and Miley builds is possible but time-consuming since their X-axes share no printed parts [1:21:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=4884s), [1:29:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=5392s).
- Upgrade advice: don't feel pressure to move from V1.5 to V1.6 before deciding on Atlas/V2 — V1.6 is a quality-of-life upgrade with no real performance gain, so skipping straight to V2 is fine if that's the plan [1:39:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=5999s), [1:40:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=6044s).

## Details worth keeping
- Panel supplier: Cut My Plastic (UK) for ACM/Dibond and polycarbonate — but avoid them for MDF/plywood cutting, go to B&Q instead [1:02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=3779s).
- Panel sizes for this Casita enclosure: roughly 670×670mm and 710×670mm side panels, plus a 550×650mm panel [1:05:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=3921s).
- Drying regime for PETG/ABS: bed at 80°C inside a Bambu P1S fitted with a humidity/heater module ("Panda Breath"), bringing the chamber to ~60°C overnight; even carbon-fiber-filled ABS needs this because the fibers (not the base polymer) absorb moisture fast [20:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=1209s), [20:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=1240s).
- End-mill rule of thumb: keep single-flute tools under ~40mm length; three-flute tools can go up to ~60mm [58:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=3482s), [58:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=3522s).
- Stream schedule change: weekend (Fri/Sat) streams pushed an hour later to better overlap with US viewers while still catching UK viewers [2:01:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USNZYueM1Is&t=7261s).
