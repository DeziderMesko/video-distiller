# Summary: Tiny Box Miley Enclosure Ep5 #millenniummachines #opensource #cnc

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA

---

## TL;DR
This is a Millennium Machines livestream build-log for "Casita," a compact CNC enclosure for their Milo router, focused almost entirely on designing a cheap three-stage air/mist filtration system (fan, filter cartridge, door seal) using scrap-bin materials like scouring pads and fish-tank foam. Along the way, the host (with LDO's Jose in chat) gives the first official Milo V2 shipping date, and there's a long detour into CNC/3D-printing enclosure noise reduction and firmware roadmap (MOS being replaced by a plugin-based "Next"). It's casual, unscripted livestream chatter with lots of real-time fabrication, tangents, and chat banter mixed in with genuinely useful engineering explanations. Best suited to people already following the Milo/Casita project rather than general CNC viewers.

## Key points
- Milo V2 will begin shipping to resellers by the end of June 2026, with pre-orders opening around mid-June — confirmed live by Jose, one of the LDO team building the kits [1:05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=3918s).
- The CNC mist-filtration cartridge uses a scrappy 3-stage design copying industrial coalescing filters: stage 1 is a stainless steel Scotch-Brite/scouring pad (coalesces oil mist back into liquid), stage 2 is open-cell foam salvaged from aquarium filters (catches remaining particulate), stage 3 is a HEPA element from a Xiaomi portable vacuum cleaner [16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=964s), [18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1090s), [19:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1140s).
- Whole filter cartridge cost under £10 to build versus commercial coalescer/filter cartridges being "really really expensive," making mist protection actually affordable to include by default [17:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1032s), [19:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1154s).
- Airflow uses 12032 radial blower fans — the same fans used in the auxiliary blower of Bambu Lab printers — chosen because radial fans generate enough static pressure to push air through the dense filter stack [10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=635s), [12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=731s).
- Argues mist inhalation is a bigger, better-understood health risk than 3D-printer VOCs, and points out most people (confirmed by chat) never actually change their Nevermore carbon filters on schedule [30:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1815s), [31:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1875s).
- Cartridge is designed to be hot-swappable and mostly washable; a planned firmware plugin will track total "misting time" and prompt a filter change around every 10 hours (rated for ~20), while the HEPA element itself needs replacing roughly every 40–50 hours at about £1 each [00:29:xx region — see below].
- MOS (Milo's current control firmware/software) is being retired in favor of "Next," a modular system where core control, 4th-axis ("Spit Roast"), and RS485 spindle control each become independent plugins [1:12:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=4378s).
- V2 files/documentation have been moved off the paid tier of Printables ("Principles" in the transcript) to be fully free on GitHub and the official website; the 4th-axis "Spit Roast" accessory remains in a closed paid beta [47:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=2832s).
- Cutter comp (compensating for actual vs. nominal tool diameter at the machine) is explained as valuable in multi-operator production shops but low-value for hobbyists doing one-off parts, and isn't currently implementable in RepRapFirmware/Duet without look-ahead support [1:18:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=4681s), [1:19:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=4769s).
- Kits will not include carbide end mills going forward (carbide pricing made it uneconomical), a step down from the well-regarded end mills bundled in past LDO kits [1:15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=4509s).
- Confirmed reseller split: Spartan3D for Eastern buyers, West3D and 3D Lab Tech for Western buyers; kits will be sold as a base V2 kit plus a separate "UEB" electronics-only bundle, with carbide/spindle sourced separately [1:12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=4347s).
- Practical noise advice for apartment/multi-residential CNC users: isolate the machine from the floor with paving stones or washer/dryer rubber mats, weatherstrip the enclosure, and just test with the door closed before assuming a downstairs neighbor will hear it [35:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=2111s), [35:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=2156s).

## Details worth keeping
- Filter stack cost breakdown: stainless scouring pad (coalescer) — pennies; aquarium filter foam (coarse stage) — about a fiver for a block, cut down; Xiaomi vacuum HEPA filter (final stage) — roughly £1 each in packs of 4–5 [17:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1032s), [19:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=1154s).
- Recommended filter maintenance cadence: change interval ~10 hours of active misting (rated ~20 hours), HEPA element every ~40–50 hours [00:29:xx-01:07:xx region — stated once, no separate bracket beyond the surrounding discussion].
- Enclosure door sealing: weatherstripping was rejected as a permanent gasket because side-to-side movement tears it over time; TPU gasket also failed on-stream (bubbled up under stress), so neither is currently the final answer [1:16:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=4578s), [1:25:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=5123s).
- Roadmap: everything currently on the Casita enclosure (electronics, filtration system) will be backported into the next alpha of the full-size "Casa" enclosure, which will deprecate most of the current Casa R3 structural design except the frame and most panels [57:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=3474s), [59:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=3595s).
- Sponsors/suppliers named: Prusa (sent them a free Core One printer — called "Core 1L" in the transcript — for testing, praised as "fantastic"), LDO, Fabreeko, West3D [50:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=3002s).
- ISOtunes were mentioned as OSHA-rated hearing-protection earbuds the host plans to buy for stream use [36:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLUO87LiPQA&t=2202s).
