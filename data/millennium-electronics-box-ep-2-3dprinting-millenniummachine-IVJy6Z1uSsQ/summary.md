# Summary: Millennium Electronics Box : Ep 2 #3dprinting #millenniummachines #opensource #filament #cncmachine

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ

---

## TL;DR
This is an unedited \~1h40m livestream from Millennium Machines (the team behind the Milo desktop CNC kit), not a produced tutorial. The host spends the stream drilling and measuring ACM side panels and redesigning the internals of Milo's electronics enclosure, while chatting with viewers about channel strategy, the automatic-tool-changer and 4th-axis roadmap, and a possible job move that could send him into van life. Substantive technical content is sparse and interspersed with long stretches of community banter (dog, D&D, age jokes, other YouTubers).

## Key points
- Drills and measures the ACM side panels for the Milo electronics box; drops the planned center mounting holes since the box sits away from the mill and won't vibrate, so they're unnecessary cost [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=487s).
- Panel hole spacing settled at 13mm and 10mm edge-to-edge; notes ACM drills more cleanly than the acrylic he normally works with [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=539s), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=990s).
- Off-stream, he redesigned the box internals: added a Raspberry Pi, reoriented the Huanyang VFD, added cable grommets, and put keystone jacks on front/back so builders can choose their own Ethernet/USB/HDMI I/O; explicitly credits Rat Rig (and Voron) as open-source inspiration for the box concept while saying Rat Rig's own box is oversized with a worse electronics layout [50:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=3026s).
- Automatic tool changer modules ("Four Pack" and "Spice Rack") have finished hardware and are mid-testing, but progress is blocked on software — the sole developer, "Nine Mile," is a new dad and struggling to finish the tool-change trigger code [55:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=3327s), [55:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=3345s).
- Fourth axis (built by teammate Phil) is close on hardware, but development has been slowed because Autodesk Fusion 360's 4-axis toolpath generation is a paid feature [1:01:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=3710s).
- A future fixed-gantry machine ("Atlas") is planned, but he's candid that burnout could kill the project — no promise it ships or that he's even still involved in a year [1:40:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=6051s), [1:41:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=6102s).
- Content strategy pivot: fewer frequent streams, more produced YouTube videos (\~weekly stream instead of every few days), after the tramming tutorial became the channel's most-watched non-announcement video; viewers agree project-style videos outperform tutorials [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=1031s), [45:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=2725s), [49:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=2955s).
- Personal update: applying for his first engineering job, which would require moving to a city; plan is to rent out his current house and live in a converted box truck ("van life") with his CNC mill and 3D printer aboard [27:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=1679s), [1:30:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=5428s).
- Explains why the base Milo kit ships with a DTI rather than a dial indicator: after a visit from LDO's Dave, he chose the cheaper option to keep the kit's price down (adding both would cost $50–100 more) [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=1031s).
- Strongly prefers flood coolant over mist coolant for steel, citing serious cancer risk from constantly inhaling atomized coolant [1:36:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=5760s).

## Details worth keeping
- Panel hole spacing: 13mm and 10mm edge-to-edge on the ACM side panels [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=539s).
- UK tool sources he uses: Cutwell (sells Insize-brand, "mid-range" tools) and Accu UK; name-checks Mitutoyo as the higher-end reference brand [20:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=1222s).
- Anecdote on marketing: gave away an old Bambu Lab A1 mini to a friend, whose casual "print of the week" posts generated more public interest in 3D printing than years of the host's own project talk [20:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=1222s).
- Expects a "Micron" desktop 3D printer kit in the next few months and plans to mill its parts on Milo [49:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=2983s).
- ADHD note: diagnosed abroad, but the UK's NHS won't accept the diagnosis — getting retested/confirmed domestically has roughly a 5-year wait, so he has no medication access [1:42:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=6162s).
- Van conversion idea: raise a box truck's floor by \~2 feet for underfloor storage, since most van conversions waste the 8–10 ft of ceiling height by leaving only \~7 ft clearance [1:30:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IVJy6Z1uSsQ&t=5428s).

## Skip it if
You want a structured tutorial or hard technical build content — this is a loosely-run livestream dominated by audience chat and personal tangents, useful mainly if you already follow Millennium Machines/Milo and care about roadmap chatter or the host's life updates.
